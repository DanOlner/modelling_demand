Modelling firm demand: live models

Interactive models that run in your browser. Nothing is installed and no data leaves the page.

  • Where GVA is made, booked and received

    A stylised flow model of a core city, a manufacturing town, a rural area and the rest of the world. It compares what official statistics show with the true flows behind them: where GVA is booked, who captures the value in supply chains, and who ends up receiving the income. Eight experiments, a scenario editor, and Webber & Huaccha's two-dimensional view of a single producer.
